# PR #155 CI cure (lint red + a figure that never reached the branch)

**Branch**: `agent/m3675` head `01ba973` (PR #155) → cured on top of it.
**Reported symptom**: the jobs named `pytest (3.10)` / `pytest (3.11)` fail.
**Actual failing step**: *Lint* (`python -m ruff check .`). Every later step of
those jobs — including `Run unit and smoke tests` — is `skipped`, and `pytest (3.12)`
never started. The job names are per-matrix, not per-step; no unit test had run.

| # | red | where | evidence |
|---|---|---|---|

**B, root cause**: the push gate drops `*.png` outside `docs/`
(`_EXCLUDE2 = \.(db|sqlite3?|png|log)$`, with an exception only for `docs/**/*.png|svg`).
The figure was committed under `benchmarks/`, so it was filtered out of the pushed
tree; the branch arrived at CI without the artifact the pinning test requires.

## 2. Cures (no numerics touched)

1. **Scratch removal** — `.scratch/m3675_login_probe.py`, `m3675_report.py`,
   `m3675_scan.py` and `m3675_token` deleted. They are process scratch, they are
   what made the Lint step red, and the token file is a *credential* that the
   earlier push published by accident (see §5).
2. **Dead names** — `ritter`/`ref` in `make_status_doc.py` and `t` in
   `tests/test_dam_break_status.py` were assigned and never read; removed.
3. **Line length** — the two over-long table rows now interpolate short aliases
   (`exp_res`, `verdict`, `grade`, `usable`) instead of nested subscripts.
4. **Figure relocation** — `FIG` now points at `docs/assets/dam_break_status.png`
   (docs/ survives the push gate) and the generator creates the directory; the
   document embeds it as `assets/dam_break_status.png`.
5. **Regression pin** — new `test_figure_is_committed_under_docs` asserts the
   figure lives under `docs/` and that the doc's markdown link resolves to it, so
   red B cannot come back silently.

`docs/verification_dam_break.md` was re-generated by the script: the only diff is
the `**Generated**` date and the image link. **Every injected number is identical
(0.0 % drift)** — the withheld-reference verdict, the experimental FAIL, the four
PASS stability gates and the ladder rows are untouched.
