## 1. What this leg adds

The ported operator is transcription-verified, but a transcription cannot carry *when* upstream calls it. Upstream applies the correction under dual-criteria time stepping -- once per **advection** step -- while the port applies it once per **acoustic** step. The ratio is `N_ac = dt_adv/dt_ac`, and the dam-break tuning of `alpha = 0.10` silently absorbs it.

The dam break cannot expose the difference (gravity and the tank walls confine the fluid). A free-floating drop can: no gravity, no wall, so nothing balances the outward surface term of the correction.

Case: `tensorsph.capillary_oscillation`, 2-D drop, free surface, no gravity, no wall, mode m = 2, R = 0.05 m, sigma = 0.072 N/m, R/dx = 8, 1.0 periods. Measured cadence ratio `N_ac = 100`.

## 3. Reading

1. At the port's cadence the same coefficient that scores best on the dam break inflates a free-floating drop without bound. The operator is not at fault -- it is transcription-verified -- the **cadence** is: the cumulative displacement available to the correction is `alpha h^2 |grad-1| x N_steps`, and `N_steps` is set by the acoustic sub-step, not by the physics.
2. What sets the size is the **cumulative budget** `alpha h^2 |grad-1| x N_steps`, not the coefficient alone: the baseline (budget 0) and the two advection-cadence runs (0.7 h and 2.9 h) straddle it, and the per-acoustic-step run (61 h) is far past it. At the port's calibrated `alpha = 0.05` the advection cadence leaves the size at the baseline value; at `alpha = 0.20` -- upstream's default -- a single application is already 0.36 h, so even the advection cadence inflates the drop. The coefficient is therefore not a property of the scheme: it is the product `alpha_adv / N_ac`, and the cadence has to be carried over, not folded into `alpha`.
3. Consequence for this anchor: `N_ac ~ 1/M` depends on the Mach number and on the grid, so a fixed per-acoustic-step `alpha` cannot transfer between cases or resolutions. This is the mechanism behind the resolution-dependence already measured on the dam break (`RESOLUTION_LADDER.md`: `alpha_opt` drifts 0.10 -> 0.15 over dx 0.04 -> 0.01, and the `alpha*dx = const` repair was falsified).
4. Actionable, and not a tuning knob: carry upstream's cadence into the solver (`tvc_every = N_ac` derived from the time-step controller) instead of absorbing the mismatch into `alpha`.

## 4. What is *not* graded here (honest scope)

* the exact-frequency gate is **not** graded: not graded at R/dx = 8 (baseline finds < 2 zero crossings in one period);
* the size channel is graded against the baseline integrator, not against a reference -- the drop's mean radius has no closed form for a particle cloud, only its *constancy* does;
* one resolution (R/dx = 8) and one coefficient pair; a resolution ladder at R/dx = 10/16/20 would settle whether the advection-cadence configuration also passes the 15 % period gate.
