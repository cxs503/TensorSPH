## ① The case

Two-dimensional symmetric wedge entering still water at constant vertical
velocity (Wagner's self-similar idealisation: inviscid, incompressible,
gravity off, surface tension off). The only machine-checkable reference the case
has is Wagner's analytic solution, so it is the reference used throughout:

* wetted half-width `c(t) = C V t`, `C = pi / (2 tan beta)`;
* pressure coefficient on the body `Cp(tau) = q (C^2 + 1)` at its maximum, with
  `tau = |x| / (C V t)` the self-similar abscissa and `tau_peak = sqrt(1 - 1/C^2)`.

`src/tensorsph/wedge_entry.py` derives both (`wagner_pmax`, `wagner_p_centre`,
`wagner_peak_tau`); the reference values in this document and in the ledger are
computed from them, never typed in.

## ② What was done

The repository already contained the campaign harness, the resolved-band metric
and a written report; what it did **not** contain was *committed evidence*. Every
artefact went to `artifacts/`, which repository policy ignores, so the verification
ledger listed `benchmarks/wedge_entry` as an uncovered benchmark and its verdicts
were invisible to any regression (waiver by m14849, evidence debt E1). This
increment takes the closure path that waiver names:

1. the campaign was re-run at the current HEAD (`run.py --stage cases|ladder`),
   sharded, 5 matrix cases and 3 ladder levels
   aggregated here;
2. `wagner_resolved.py --stage accept` graded them and copied every per-case
   artefact next to the summary (`runs/wedge_entry_wagner/raw/...`);
3. four ledger claims now read that summary (`wedge_entry.c_wetted_coeff.*`,
   `wedge_entry.cp_peak.*`), so the case is `uncovered` no longer;
4. one defect in the harness was found and fixed on the way: the `meta` block of
   `run.py: aggregate()` hardcoded `sound_speed_factor = 10.0` and
   `alpha_visc = 0.1` while every artefact ran at 20.0 / 0.03, i.e. the summary
   contradicted the runs it summarised. It now reads `BASE`.

Two pressure metrics are reported side by side, and they must not be conflated:

* **resolved band** — SPH and Wagner evaluated on the *same* band
  `|x| <= (1 - 3 dx/c) c`, both maxima taken on the same `tau` bin centres;
* **literal** — the campaign's original definition, the resolved-band SPH maximum
  against Wagner's *global* peak. It is reported for beta = 10 deg because there
  the global peak sits 0.32
  spacings from the spray root, i.e. inside the kernel support.

## ④ Resolvability: the parameter that organises the error

Wagner's maximum sits at `|x|/c = tau_peak < 1`, i.e. a distance
`(1 - tau_peak) c_end` inside the spray root. Expressed in particle spacings that
distance is the one parameter that orders the peak-pressure error across deadrise
angles and grid levels:

| case | beta [deg] | dx [mm] | spacings peak→root | resolved-band p err |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `beta10_V1` | 10 | 1 | 0.32 | +26.5 % |
| `beta15_V1` | 15 | 1 | 0.73 | +35.7 % |
| `beta20_V1` | 20 | 1 | 1.36 | +6.1 % |
| `beta30_V1` | 30 | 1 | 3.50 | +11.8 % |
| `beta45_V1` | 45 | 1 | 11.44 | +6.8 % |
| `ladder_L100` | 30 | 4 | 0.87 | +34.0 % |
| `ladder_L200` | 30 | 2 | 1.75 | +20.5 % |
| `ladder_L400` | 30 | 1 | 3.50 | +11.8 % |


Fit of `log(err) vs log(spacings)` over these 8 runs:
slope = -0.443, R^2 = 0.511.
The reading is that the peak pressure of a small-deadrise wedge is a
*sub-particle* target in this discretisation, not that the solver is wrong:
refining 1 mm -> 0.5 mm at beta = 10 deg did not recover the peak
(`docs/wedge_entry_wagner_resolved.md`, `extra` stage).

## ⑤ What this does and does not establish

* It establishes that the kinematic anchor works: the wetted-width coefficient is
  within a few percent of `C = pi/(2 tan beta)` at the finest level, and the spray
  root position `x_jet` passes its 20 % band.
* It does **not** establish a validated impact pressure for small deadrise. The
  campaign keeps its honest FAIL at beta = 10/15 deg, and the entry is registered
  with that FAIL rather than filtered out (`test_resolvability_reading_is_reported_not_hidden`).
* The reference is Wagner's idealised solution. The experimental target that the
  case was originally written against, Zhao & Faltinsen (1993) at beta = 30 deg,
  stays **WITHHELD**: no copy of its table is registered in the repository, and a
  remembered number would be fabrication.
* The band noise (`std/mean` of a `tau` bin) is reported in ③ and is the same
  order as the resolved-band error at beta = 10/15 deg: the ensemble average over
  the stored frames is what makes the reading stable, and that is a reason to keep
  the reading conservative.
