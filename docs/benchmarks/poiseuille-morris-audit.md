## Why the cancellation check exists

The scored band `0.15 ≤ y/H ≤ 0.85` is blind to a profile that is too flat in the core and too fast near the walls: the row errors alternate in sign, the RMS collapses, and a `PASS` appears. The audit reports, per case, the largest error on the rows the band *excludes* and the fraction of scored rows whose error sign opposes the centre row. The WCSPH baseline's 2.76 % at `Re_c = 12.26`, `H/dx = 24` is exactly that pattern, so it is recorded as a qualified (cancellation) pass, not as accuracy.
